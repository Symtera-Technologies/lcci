## 2. Chamber-by-chamber findings

### 2.1 Dubai Chambers
**Main menu:** About · Business Hub · Services · Family Businesses · Knowledge Centre · What's On

**Online services (11):** Membership, Certificate of Origin, Attestation, ATA Carnet, Mediation, Venue Booking, Document Verification, Information Letters, Business Council renewal, ESG Label, Commercial Directory

**Standout features**
- Mobile app (iOS, Android, Huawei) with a dashboard and application tracking
- "Let's Chat" widget, WhatsApp and an AI assistant
- English/Arabic toggle, site search and a login portal
- "Happiness Meter" feedback link
- Impact statistics section
- Switcher to sister chambers' sites
- Warning notice about AI and deepfake fraud

**Design:** full-width banners, service cards with icons, large statistic counters, dropdown menus

### 2.2 London Chamber of Commerce and Industry
**Main menu:** What we offer · Events · Business Groups · Policy & Campaigns · International · News & Insights · About
**Utility links:** Log in · Join · Search · Contact

**Homepage sections (in order):** news ticker → hero with "Become a Member" → premium membership promo → advocacy campaign (Choose LDN.) → members' lounge → magazine carousel → "What can we do to help?" (9 service tiles) → key figures → membership overview → Connect / Champion / Support → patron logos → events → testimonials → join call to action → news

**Online services**
- Online join, member login and account area
- Events calendar with "Book Now" and a webinar library
- Trade documents: Certificates of Origin, ATA Carnet, EUR1, Customs Declarations, Legalisation (up to 50% off for members)
- Arbitration and mediation, venue finder, member offers

**Standout features**
- **Hubs:** Business Information, Ask the Expert, Access to Finance, Procurement, Skills & Employment
- **Groups:** 9 sector groups, plus clubs such as Women in Business and the Asian Business Association
- **Publications:** Quarterly Economic Survey and the *London Business Matters* magazine
- **Key figures on the homepage:** 12,000+ network, 200+ events a year, 140+ years

**Design:** image-led layout, cards and tiles, repeated calls to action, and a skip-to-content link for accessibility

### 2.7 Karachi Chamber of Commerce & Industry (KCCI): local benchmark
**Main menu:** Renewal 2026-27 · About KCCI · Research · Media Centre · Events · Members' Area · Contact

**Online services:** new membership and renewal, **document attestation**, **visa recommendation letter**, active members directory, voter lists

**Standout features:** WhatsApp channel with QR code, Daily E-Bulletin, *Economic Horizon* publication, budget recommendations, impact statistics

**Gaps seen:** statistics display as "0", inconsistent business hours, logo alt text left over from a template ("Porto"); functional but not modern

**Relevance:** KCCI already offers online attestation and visa letters. LCCI needs at least these to stay on par.

## 4. Recommendations for LCCI

### 4.1 Structure
- **Six menus:** About · Membership · Services · Advocacy & Research · Trade & Events · Newsroom
- **Utility bar:** Smart Services · Member Login · Join · Search · اردو
- A **"Smart Services"** entry point on every page (Dubai, Abu Dhabi model)
- **Topic pages** (Tax & Budget, Energy, Exports, IT & AI, SMEs) that gather news, positions and circulars on one subject (US Chamber model)

### 4.2 Features (priority order)
1. **Member portal:** join, renew and pay online; request certificates of origin, attestation and visa letters; track status
2. **Smart directory:** filter by sector and business type, verified badges, inquiry form
3. **Events:** calendar, online booking, webinar and video library
4. **Economy dashboard:** exports, inflation, KIBOR, PKR/USD and LCCI survey results
5. **Alerts and chat:** WhatsApp channel, circular alerts by sector, and an AI help desk answering from LCCI content
6. **Trade and investment:** "Doing Business in Lahore" guide, trade inquiries, exhibitions and fairs calendar
7. **Committee pages:** each standing committee with its mandate, members and activities
8. **Trust and access:** English/Urdu, accessibility controls, scam warning, privacy policy
9. **Publications:** a monthly *Lahore Business* newsletter or magazine, research reports, budget proposals
10. **Later phase:** a mobile app using the same CMS and portal

### 4.3 Homepage blueprint
1. Notice strip (dated, expires automatically)
2. Hero: one message, plus Join and Smart Services buttons
3. Quick service tiles: Renew · Certificate of Origin · Attestation · Circulars · Directory
4. Key figures: members, years since founding, events per year, sectors [LCCI to provide]
5. Latest news (dated, with photos) and upcoming events
6. Advocacy spotlight: current campaign or budget proposals
7. Economy at a glance (indicator strip)
8. Committees and sector groups
9. Partners and sponsors (tiered logos)
10. Member testimonials
11. Newsletter sign-up and WhatsApp channel
12. Footer: contact, departments, quick links, social, legal

### 4.4 Design direction
- Clean card layout with large real photos of LCCI events; **no pop-ups or auto-sliders**
- One brand colour plus an accent; consistent icon set; dates on every item
- Large statistic counters and clear, repeated calls to action
- Mobile first, fast, readable; right-to-left Urdu layout
- Professional photography and a consistent visual identity across the web, newsletter and social media
